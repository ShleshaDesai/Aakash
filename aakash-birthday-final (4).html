The Letter

From Shlesha, to Aakash. Tap play.

Happy Birthday, Aakash. I don't think you fully know what you've been to me this year. My biggest cheerleader. My biggest supporter. The person who somehow makes ordinary nights feel like something worth remembering. I loved when we used to go out clubbing or drinking, dancing until we were soaked and laughing about it after. And I loved just as much going out with your friends — you always made me feel included, celebrated, like I belonged in every room you walked into. It's the small efforts that are everything to me. You are the smile I wear, the tears I cry, and the happiness I feel. But this is the part I actually want you to sit with: I don't know what the future holds for us. Nobody ever really does. But I know this — whatever it looks like, however it unfolds, I will always be there for you in any way I can. That's not conditional on anything working out perfectly. That's just who I am when it comes to you. I try, every single day, to understand you a little better and to be a little better for you. Not because you've asked me to, but because you deserve someone who's actually trying. And I need you to know this too — I want you to achieve everything you have ever wanted. Every goal, every dream, every version of the life you're building. And I know, without a doubt, that you will get there. Even if I'm not standing next to you when you do. That's how much I believe in you. Our movie nights, the ones where you get so excited to show me the films you love — I hope we get a hundred more of those. But even on the nights I'm not there, I hope some part of you knows I'm still cheering, from wherever I am. I'm sorry, too. I know I can be difficult to be with, and I am extremely sorry if my insecurities have ever made you feel anxious or unappreciated. Just know that I am trying to be better, step by step. I've never been able to put into words how grateful I am to have you. Here's to you, Aakash. All of you. I love you.
